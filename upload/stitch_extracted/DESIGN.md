## Brand & Style
This design system establishes an elevated, editorial digital experience tailored for high-end personal portfolios, curated archives, and bespoke creative showcases. The design language balances high-fashion publishing aesthetics with architectural precision, evoking quiet confidence, deliberate curation, and tactile craftsmanship.

The visual style blends refined Minimalism with warm tactile editorial undertones. It discards heavy dropshadows, loud gradients, and hyperactive micro-interactions in favor of considered negative space, deliberate typographic tension, hairline borders, and subtle tonal depth reminiscent of luxury archival print and uncoated heavy-stock paper.

## Colors
The color palette draws directly from organic botanical dyes, dark slate strata, and warm architectural terracotta, grounded against an ivory paper ground. 

- **Primary (`#133D2D`)**: A rich, deep botanical green used for key focal accents, authoritative headings, and high-impact structural anchors.
- **Secondary (`#D35A38`)**: An earthy terracotta/coral that brings warmth, life, and selective focus to interactive states, key tags, and callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#2E3B35`)**: Deep muted forest slate bridging the neutral background and primary accents for supporting metadata, subheadings, and icons.
- **Neutral (`#FAF7F2`)**: Warm ivory/cream reminiscent of fine uncoated paper, eliminating the stark, clinical glare of pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Surface & Borders**: Dividers and hairline strokes rely on `border-subtle` (`#E5DFD7`) to structure content with whispering contrast rather than visual clutter.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy relies on high dynamic contrast between the literary refinement of **Newsreader** and the modernist clarity of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader)**: Deployed for display titles, project case studies, editorial quotes, and section headers. Its graceful letterforms and calligraphic proportion establish an intellectual, gallery-level quality. Use italic cuts sparingly to underscore artistic direction and project years.
- **Structural Sans (Manrope)**: Handles navigation, body prose, specifications, and UI badges. Its geometric construction keeps dense information legible, crisp, and unobtrusive.
- **Label Tracking**: All metadata tags, status pills, and system subtitles use `label-caps` styled with uppercase casing and intentional tracking (+0.08em) to anchor the editorial magazine grid.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a modular 12-column architectural grid with generous margins and breathing room to honor content curation.

- **Desktop Grid (1024px and up)**: 12-column structure with `2rem` (32px) gutters and a max-width container of `1440px`. Page margins default to `4rem` (64px). Asymmetrical column spans (e.g., 7 cols for hero media paired with 5 cols for project narration) are preferred to break template symmetry.
- **Tablet Grid (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` horizontal margins.
- **Mobile Grid (up to 767px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Complex masonry cards collapse into an uncluttered, single-column vertical flow.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing scales on an intentional exponential curve: tight micro-spacing (`0.25rem`–`0.75rem`) within cards and metadata, shifting into expansive structural spacing (`4rem`–`9rem`) between editorial narratives and gallery sections.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system rejects heavy drop shadows in favor of editorial flatness, soft tonal planes, and delicate hairline boundaries:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Tonal Shifts**: Depth is established by placing ivory cards (`#F3EFEA`) over the softer canvas ground (`#FAF7F2`), bounded by crisp 1px borders in `border-subtle` (`#E5DFD7`).
- **Tactile Shadows (Hover Only)**: For interactive gallery cards and floating header navigation, elevation is indicated via an ultra-soft, diffused slate shadow: `0 8px 30px -6px rgba(22, 27, 24, 0.05)`.
- **Layer Stacking**: Elevated elements (such as persistent bottom drawer navigation or detail modals) use clean tonal backdrops with a gentle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at 90% opacity, allowing imagery beneath to whisper through.

## Shapes
Shapes emphasize tailored elegance with a disciplined `Soft` roundedness philosophy (`0.25rem` base):

- **Interactive Cards & Panels**: Use subtle `0.25rem` to `0.375rem` corners, preserving architectural crispness without feeling clinical or blunt.
- **Badges & Pills**: Status pills, category tags, and secondary action buttons adopt fully circular `9999px` radii, creating a deliberate visual tension against the rectangular boundaries of the masonry grid.
- **Imagery**: Project photography remains strictly squared or subtly softened at `0.25rem`, maintaining the integrity of the captured work without intrusive ornamentation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid deep slate background (`#161B18`) or forest green (`#133D2D`) with cream text (`#FAF7F2`). Border radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`, font `Manrope` medium 14px. On hover, shifts softly to terracotta (`#D35A38`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill with a 1px border in `border-subtle` (`#E5DFD7`), dark charcoal text. On hover, gains a soft ivory tint (`#F3EFEA`) with `border-slate-dark`.
- **Understated Action**: Text-only button with a persistent 1px underline positioned 4px below the baseline; transitions to terracotta with a slight right-shift on hover.

### Badges & Filter Chips
- **Pill Badges**: Compact pill form factor (`rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`). Background in muted sage (`#E2E8E3`) or card ivory (`#F3EFEA`) with text in `label-caps` in deep forest (`#133D2D`).
- **Filter Chips**: Neutral ivory background with delicate border. Selected state inverts to charcoal-slate (`#161B18`) background with cream text.

### Interactive Masonry Project Gallery Cards
- **Structure**: Unconstrained aspect ratios accommodating editorial portrait, landscape, and panoramic documentation.
- **Styling**: Rendered in card-ivory (`#F3EFEA`) with 1px border (`#E5DFD7`). Image sits flush or nested with `1.25rem` internal matte padding.
- **Typography & Details**: Project title rendered in `headline-sm` (Newsreader), followed by an uppercase category stamp in `label-caps` (Manrope) and year.
- **Interaction**: Subtle image scale (1.02x scale within an `overflow-hidden` frame) over 400ms ease-out on hover, paired with the faint diffused elevation shadow.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Fields**: Architectural minimal underlines or flat inset boxes (`#FAF7F2` with 1px base border). Focus state transitions border color seamlessly to deep forest (`#133D2D`) without harsh glow rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square or circular frames with 1px stroke. Active state filled with primary forest green, containing a crisp white tick mark.

### Editorial Lists & Index Tables
- **Archive Row**: Alternating rows separated by 1px hairline horizontal dividers (`#E5DFD7`). Features column alignments for Year, Project Name, Discipline, and Client, using `body-sm` and `label-caps`. Rows illuminate with a warm ivory background shift on mouseover.
